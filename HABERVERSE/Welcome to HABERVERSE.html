Welcome to HABERVERSE

This is your own cloud. Every document you write here is a file in your HABERVERSE folder, and it saves by itself while you type.

Try it

  • Open HABERVERSE
  • Press New and start a document
  • Drag a few files or a whole folder onto the window
  • Drag a document onto a folder to move it

Good to know

  • Right-click anything to rename, copy, download or delete it.
  • Click the arrow at the top left of a document to come back here.
  • You can delete this document whenever you like.
